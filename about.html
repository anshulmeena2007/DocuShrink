Our Story & Philosophy

About DocuHug

Simple tools for your files. Private by design.

Why DocuHug Exists

Every day, millions of students, job seekers, professionals, and creators need to perform simple file operations: compressing a scanned ID card under 100 KB, resizing a portrait photograph, converting an image to WebP, or merging several PDF documents into a single application packet.

Unfortunately, most file conversion websites on the internet force users to upload their private, sensitive documents to remote servers. This introduces real security concerns, slow server upload queues, daily file limits, and aggressive paywalls.

DocuHug was created as a modern alternative: a collection of clean, fast, and entirely client-side browser utilities where file processing occurs directly on your own device.

100% In-Browser Execution

Your files never travel across the network to our servers. All rendering, compression, format encoding, and PDF compilation take place locally in your device's memory.

Instant Local Speed

Zero waiting for server upload queues or bandwidth throttling. Processing starts the instant you drop your file and finishes in milliseconds.

No Accounts or Sign-Ups

No registration forms, password creation, or email collection. Open the tool you need, process your files, and get on with your day.

True File Security

Because files are never transmitted to our backend, they cannot be intercepted in transit, leaked from a server database, or accessed by third parties.

Supported Workflows

DocuHug currently provides 10 dedicated client-side utilities across two core categories:

Image Utilities:

  • Compress Image (JPG, PNG, WebP with ~50KB presets)
  • Resize Image (Pixel dimensions & aspect ratio lock)
  • Convert Image (Cross-format conversion with transparency)
  • Crop Image (Interactive frame with 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 presets)
  • Image to PDF (Multi-image compilation with page sorting)
  • JPG to PDF (Standardized printable document export)

PDF Utilities:

  • Merge PDF (Combine multiple files with drag reordering)
  • Split PDF (Extract page ranges or split every page into ZIP)
  • PDF to JPG (High-resolution Canvas page rasterizer)
  • Compress PDF (Stream object cleaning & downsampling)
