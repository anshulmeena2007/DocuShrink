How Browser-Based Image Compression Works

DocuHug processes image compression locally inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and native encoder algorithms. When you adjust the quality slider or pick a preset, your device decodes the pixel raster, re-encodes the image at the selected compression level, and produces a clean Blob in your browser's local memory.

Understanding Target-Size Presets (~50 KB, ~100 KB, ~200 KB)

Many government portals, university exam forms, and job applications enforce strict maximum file size thresholds (such as 50 KB or 100 KB). Our intelligent binary-search solver iteratively determines the ideal balance of lossy encoding parameters and resolution scaling to match your target file size without causing blurry artifacts.

Complete Privacy Guarantee

Because no network requests are sent to an image upload server, your personal photos, ID documents, signatures, and confidential receipts remain strictly on your local device.
