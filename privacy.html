Transparency & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

DocuHug is dedicated to providing simple, powerful file utilities while honoring the highest standards of user privacy. Our platform was architected with a fundamental principle: your documents and images belong to you and should remain on your own device.

Unlike traditional file conversion services that require you to upload files to a remote cloud server for processing, DocuHug operates entirely through modern client-side browser technologies.

2. How File Processing Works (100% Client-Side)

When you select or drop images or PDF documents into any DocuHug tool:

  • Local Memory Execution: Files are read directly into your browser's local sandbox runtime using standard Web APIs (such as the HTML5 File API, Canvas API, and Web Workers).
  • Zero Server Uploads: Your files are never transmitted across the internet to a DocuHug server or any third-party computing infrastructure.
  • No Cloud Storage or Retention: Because files never reach our servers, we have no mechanism, database, or desire to view, store, copy, or retain your documents.
  • Local Downloads: Generated output files (compressed images, converted formats, cropped photos, and assembled PDFs) are constructed in browser memory as local Blob objects and saved directly to your device.

3. Information We Process

DocuHug does not require user registration, login credentials, passwords, or personal identity information to use any core file tool.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details for using our file utilities.
  • File Metadata: Document names and dimensions are inspected locally by your browser solely to render previews and configure processing parameters.
  • Analytics & Telemetry: DocuHug does not run intrusive user behavior tracking scripts.

4. Third-Party Libraries & CDNs

To deliver client-side file manipulation, DocuHug loads open-source JavaScript libraries (such as PDF.js, pdf-lib, and JSZip) and styling frameworks from trusted Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including Cloudflare cdnjs.

When your web browser downloads these static JavaScript and CSS files, the CDN provider may log standard HTTP request metadata (such as your IP address and user-agent string) to ensure uptime, security, and content delivery performance in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Advertising & Future Third-Party Providers

To maintain free access to our tools, DocuHug features reserved non-intrusive advertising placements. When advertising networks (such as Google AdSense or compliant ad exchanges) are activated:

  • Third-party ad vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or device identifiers to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings (google.com/settings/ads) or through industry opt-out portals such as aboutads.info.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

DocuHug's core file processing engine does not use tracking cookies or persistent local database storage to identify individual visitors. Temporary in-memory Object URLs (`blob:`) created during file manipulation are automatically revoked when you close the tab, reset your workspace, or remove files.

7. Security of Your Files

Because all file operations take place locally on your computer or mobile device, the security of your files relies on the security of your personal device and web browser. We recommend keeping your web browser updated to the latest version to ensure current security patches.

8. Children's Privacy

DocuHug does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our service does not collect personal identifiers from any user, our tools are safe for general audiences of all ages.

9. External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites or documentation. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy of every website you visit, as we have no control over third-party content and practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological improvements or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of the policy.
